About Me

Who I Am

My name is Cayman Archer, and I was born and raised in Las Vegas, Nevada. Growing up in a city built around people, entertainment, and constant change taught me to pay attention to what makes people tick and how they make decisions. That curiosity has followed me into everything I do, from the classes I choose to the projects I take on.

What I Study

I am a Digital Culture and Data Analytics major with minors in both Psychology and Business. Each piece of my education shapes how I approach a problem: data analytics gives me the tools to find patterns and tell stories with numbers, psychology helps me understand the people behind those numbers, and business grounds my work in real-world goals and outcomes.

What I love most is combining my passion for helping people with data analysis. I believe good data work is not just about charts and spreadsheets. It is about asking the right questions, listening to the people affected, and turning information into something clear and useful that helps others make better decisions.

Outside the Classroom

In my spare time, you can usually find me on the tennis court. Tennis keeps me active and has taught me a lot about focus, patience, and bouncing back after a tough point. When I am off the court, I am probably winding down with a good sitcom. I am always happy to trade recommendations for a new show to watch.
